How this is calculated

Order: (1) NIS on gross, capped at the annual ceiling ÷ 12  →  (2) Education Tax on gross − NIS − pension  →  (3) NHT on full gross, uncapped  →  (4) chargeable income = gross − NIS − pension − threshold  →  (5) PAYE at the low rate up to the high-band cutoff, remainder at the high rate.

The income tax threshold moves every April 1 — the default here (J$1,902,360/yr) is the rate effective April–December 2026. Edit the assumptions panel for the January–March 2026 threshold (J$1,799,376) or any future year. This is a planning estimate, not filing advice — confirm against TAJ before running payroll.
